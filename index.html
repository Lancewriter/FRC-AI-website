Sectors in Focus

Tools for SMEs

Comprehensive solutions tailored for different industry sectors

Construction Companies

  • Construction companies often grapple with irregular cashflows due to delayed payments from clients, unforeseen project overruns, and the need to evaluate the creditworthiness of clients and the solvency of banking partners.
  • FRAICLY provides data-driven tools to optimize cash flow, assess client credit risk, evaluate banking partner reliability, and deliver actionable insights to streamline operations, reduce financial uncertainty, and support sustainable growth.

Non Finance Consultancies

  • Non Finance Consultancies such as marketing agencies, legal consultancies, and IT firms often face irregular cashflows due to delayed client payments, project overruns, or unpredictable demand.
  • FRAICLY offers methodologies and tools to forecast cashflow, evaluate client creditworthiness, assess banking partner reliability, and deliver actionable insights to streamline operations, mitigate risks, and drive sustainable growth.

Manufacturing and Corporates

  • Manufacturing companies and corporates often struggle with supply chain disruptions, client credit risks, depository bank solvency, rising costs, and maintaining cashflow in volatile economic conditions.
  • FRAICLY delivers data-driven tools to optimize cashflow, assess client creditworthiness, evaluate bank solvency, and provide actionable insights to enhance operational efficiency, mitigate risks, and drive sustainable growth.
  • FRAICLY also offers finance projects that provide competitive advantages and financial resilience. We help businesses prepare for economic shocks.

Fin-techs and High Growth Financial Services

Financiers

  • Fintechs and growth-stage finance providers often encounter challenges like managing liquidity constraints, evaluating client creditworthiness, mitigating default risks, and scaling operations efficiently in a rapidly evolving market.
  • FRAICLY delivers advanced financial analytics, risk assessment tools, and AI-driven insights to optimize liquidity, evaluate client solvency, reduce credit risks, and enhance decision-making for sustainable growth and operational scalability.

Insurers

  • Insurtech and startup-stage insurers or brokers often struggle with managing underwriting risks, ensuring reinsurance partner reliability, maintaining liquidity, and scaling operations efficiently while navigating regulatory complexities.
  • FRAICLY provides advanced risk assessment tools, evaluates reinsurance solvency, optimizes liquidity management, and delivers AI-driven insights to enhance decision-making, streamline operations, and support scalable growth in a competitive market.
  • FRAICLY can also provide guidance on optimising IFRS 17 and other finance projects.

Hospitality and Restaurants

Hotels

  • Hotels often struggle with unpredictable cashflow, inefficient operations, rising costs, and difficulty in making data-driven decisions to optimize pricing, manage resources, and meet customer expectations.
  • FRAICLY leverages advanced financial tools and AI-driven insights to optimize cashflow, streamline operations, enhance pricing strategies, reduce inefficiencies, and provide actionable customer and market intelligence for sustainable growth.

Restaurants

  • Restaurants face challenges such as managing fluctuating demand, controlling food costs, minimizing waste, ensuring consistent cashflow, and delivering personalized customer experiences in a competitive market.
  • FRAICLY provides AI-driven tools to optimize menu pricing, forecast demand, streamline inventory management, reduce waste, and analyze customer preferences, enabling restaurants to increase profitability and enhance operational efficiency.
  • FRAICLY's Partnerships: If your restaurant/club is lacking footfall we can introduce you to our partners that specialize in social media marketing campaigns.

Non Profits

  • Non-profits often face inconsistent funding streams, delayed donor contributions, and challenges in evaluating the financial reliability of donors or banking partners.
  • FRAICLY offers tailored solutions to forecast cashflow, assess donor creditworthiness, evaluate banking partner solvency, and provide actionable insights to optimize resource allocation, enhance financial stability, and ensure sustainable impact.
